# Reviewed operational extract: FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26
This is a normalized implementation extract, NOT a verbatim copy of legislation.
Authority: Income Tax Department AY 2025-26 salaried-individual guidance;
Income-tax Act 1961 sections 10(13A), 16(ia), 24(b), 71(3A), 80C/80CCE,
80CCD(1B), 80D, 87A, 115BAC, 288A and 288B; Income-tax Rules Rule 2A;
Finance (No.2) Act 2024. Exact source URLs are in SOURCES.md.

Scope: individual, salary only, optionally eligible self-occupied home interest.
No capital gains, business income, other income, TDS/advance-tax credits,
professional tax, employer NPS, arrears relief, or other deductions. Currency INR.
Age is age at end of financial year. Residency is tax residency, not nationality.

OLD: Resident under 60 / all non-residents: first 250000 at 0%, next to 500000
at 5%, next to 1000000 at 20%, remaining 30%.
Resident age 60-79: first 300000 exempt, next to 500000 at 5%, next to
1000000 at 20%, remaining 30%. Resident age >=80: first 500000 exempt,
next to 1000000 at 20%, remaining 30%.
NEW: first 300000 at 0%, 300000-700000 at 5%, 700000-1000000 at 10%,
1000000-1200000 at 15%, 1200000-1500000 at 20%, above 1500000 at 30%.
New rates are independent of age and residency.

16(ia): salary standard deduction lesser of salary and 50000 (old), 75000 (new).
10(13A)/Rule 2A: old HRA exemption = max(0, min(actual HRA received,
rent paid minus 10% HRA salary, 50% HRA salary in Delhi/Mumbai/Kolkata/
Chennai or 40% elsewhere)). HRA salary includes basic plus qualifying DA
and fixed-percentage turnover commission, for the relevant rented period.
The input must be for a single consistent period. Changing periods require
separate HRA calculations, which the current parser must clarify/escalate.
Salary income cannot be negative from HRA plus standard deduction.
Old 80C eligible amounts capped at 150000, 80CCE aggregate applies.
Old 80CCD(1B) eligible ADDITIONAL NPS capped at 50000; no double counting.
Old 80D: eligible insurance premiums (not cash); self/spouse/dependent
children group capped at 25000, 50000 if eligible resident senior in group;
parents independently capped at 25000, or 50000 for eligible resident senior.
Preventive check-up aggregate <=5000 across both groups, within group caps.
Eligible non-cash medical expenditure for uninsured resident seniors may
replace premiums within 50000 group cap. Do not count both premium and
medical expenditure for the same insured person. Implementation conservatively
requires separate uninsured groups and rejects mixed insurance/expense groups.
Old self-occupied home interest: 24(b) capped at 200000 if acquisition/
construction borrowing >=1 April 1999 and statutory completion conditions
(including five-year completion period where applicable) met, otherwise 30000.
Require eligibility confirmation. House-property loss set-off <=200000.
New regime disallows the above HRA, 80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D and self-occupied
interest claims. No unsupported deductions may be silently assumed.

288A: after deductions, clamp total income to >=0; discard paise then round
to nearest 10 (last digit >=5 rounds up). Compute all tax on that income.
87A OLD: residents with total income <=500000 rebate min(tax,12500).
No old-regime marginal rebate just above 500000.
87A NEW: residents with total income <=700000 rebate min(tax,25000).
The actual slab tax at 700000 for this year is 20000, so effective rebate
cannot exceed that actual tax. Above 700000, marginal relief reduces tax
to the excess income where slab tax exceeds income minus 700000.
No rebate/marginal rebate for non-residents.

Surcharge based on total income, applied to tax after rebate:
income >5000000:10%; >10000000:15%; >20000000:25%; >50000000:37% OLD only.
NEW maximum surcharge 25%. Exact thresholds do not enter the next band.
At each increase in rate, marginal relief caps income tax plus surcharge at
income tax plus the previous surcharge at that threshold + excess income.
Cess 4% on income tax + surcharge - marginal relief, in both regimes.
288B: payable after cess: discard paise, then nearest 10, ties up.
Expose unrounded payable too; do not confuse income tax with TDS balance.
